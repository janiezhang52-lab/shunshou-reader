# Third-party notices and original-program license boundaries

[简体中文](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) | English

ECDICT definitions, ipa-dict American IPA, cmudict-ipa, and syllabify retain their original MIT licenses. CMUdict retains its accompanying redistribution notice. Original texts are in [extension/licenses/](extension/licenses/); pinned versions and input SHA-256 values are in [metadata.json](extension/data/metadata.json).

The project's [ECDICT authorization review](ECDICT-AUTHORIZATION.en.md) records the maintainer's replies of 2019-04-19 and 2026-09-30 concerning data use and bundled offline lookup. It describes reliance on project-level permission while acknowledging that the historical third-party rights chain has not been independently checked for every entry. That gap is not itself a finding of infringement or a license exception.

The root [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers only original code and documentation the author has the right to license. Its restrictions on selling copies and publicly distributing modifications do not apply to independently licensed third-party MIT data. Retain third-party copyright, license, and CMUdict notices. Rights lawfully obtained under an earlier MIT version of the original program are not revoked.

This explanatory translation does not amend any license. The original Chinese custom license remains authoritative; an English legal translation requires separate confirmation.
